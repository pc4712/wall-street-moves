About Wall Street Moves

Wall Street Moves is an independent publication covering U.S. markets, stocks, AI and technology, earnings, and the Federal Reserve.

What we cover

We report on the forces moving American markets: index moves and market breadth, individual stocks and sectors, the AI infrastructure buildout, quarterly earnings, and Federal Reserve policy. Our coverage is organized into five sections: U.S. Markets, Stock Analysis, AI & Technology, Earnings, and Economy & Fed.

How we work

Every article on this site is original writing based on verifiable facts. Market prices and index levels come from market-close data. Company figures come from company earnings releases and regulatory filings. Policy figures come from the Federal Reserve and the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Analyst views are attributed to the named analyst and firm, with the date of the view. Each article lists its sources, states when it was last updated, and carries a short fact-check note explaining how its figures were verified.

We do not invent prices, quotes, analyst views, author names, or publication dates. When a claim cannot be verified against a real source, we leave it out. When a source is an estimate — for example, an interpolated inflation figure or a market-implied probability — we say so.

What we are not

Wall Street Moves is not a broker, an investment adviser, or a research firm. Nothing on this site is investment advice, an offer, or a solicitation to buy or sell any security. See our Disclaimer for the full statement.

Corrections

If you find an error in our reporting, please tell us through the Contact page. We correct factual errors promptly and note the correction on the article.
